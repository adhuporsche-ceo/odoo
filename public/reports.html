Academic & Mentoring Reports

Export official CSV data reports for department reviews, academic audits, and career guidance meetings.

Privacy Compliance: All generated reports strictly conform to college privacy regulations. Sensitive family and parent income details are never included in bulk export documents.
Output Format: Comma Separated Values (.csv)
Students Directory Report
General Master Profile List

Exports student register numbers, names, departments, sections, categories, contact emails, current cumulative CGPA, and career goals.

Academic Performance Report
Semester-wise SGPA & CGPA Breakdown

Exports semester-by-semester SGPA, cumulative CGPA, attendance percentages, arrear flags, and recorded student academic achievements.

Arrear Management & Remediation Report
Pending and Cleared Arrear History

Detailed subject-wise arrear occurrences, number of exam attempts, current pending/cleared status, cleared semesters, clearing grades, and remedial coaching flags.

Career Goals & Aspirations Report
Placement, Higher Studies & Startups

Summary of student career goal choices, target company types, salary expectations, target foreign universities/exams, or entrepreneurship venture stages.

Mentor Interventions & Counseling Log Report
Official Mentoring Session Logs & Follow-up Dates

Exports logged faculty counseling sessions, meeting dates, intervention reasons, actions taken, assigned student mentors, and follow-up deadlines.
