Mentoring Attention Thresholds

These thresholds define transparent, rule-based criteria for flagging students in the Mentor Attention Center without subjective bias.

% and below
Students with semester attendance below 75% are automatically flagged for counseling.
CGPA and below
Students with CGPA below 6.5 receive proactive remedial support.
Thresholds can be customized by administrators in the .env configuration file (ATTENDANCE_ATTENTION_THRESHOLD, CGPA_ATTENTION_THRESHOLD).
